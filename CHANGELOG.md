## 2026-11-21 — Pantry usable units: dual usable + purchase display, whole usable-unit editing, 4-decimal precision & smallest-usable-unit deductions (`app.js` `v=20261121a`, `styles.css` `v=20261121a`, `index.html`)

- **What changed (Neon-built from Yidi's approved `BUILD-SPEC.md`, Nov 20, 2026):**
  1. **Usable-unit resolver (`pantryUsableUnit`, `parsePantryCountPackUnit`, `lookupUnitConversion`):** Resolves countable usable units from `data/unit-conversions.json` without modifying stored lot data. Triggers strictly on:
     - Count-pack lot units (`sleeve`, `dozen`, `pack`, `box`/`pack_name`, `carton` for eggs, or `parsePantryCountPackUnit` `/^(\d+)[\s-]*(ct|count|pk|pack)s?$/i`) when the table defines both `units_per_pack` and `pack_unit` (count taken from the unit string, fallback `conv.units_per_pack` — v1: garlic sleeves → 5 heads, eggs `"12 ct"`/`dozen` → 12 eggs, romaine `"3 ct"`/`"3-pack"` → 3 heads, butter/margarine `box` → 4 sticks; cheese slices excluded because they have no `pack_unit`); OR
     - Explicit countable-by-weight: garlic / fresh garlic in `lb`/`oz` (`lb_per_head` + `cloves_per_head`, 10 heads/lb or 0.625 heads/oz, sub-unit `clove` at 10/head).
     - Never triggers on raw `lb_per_unit` (chicken cutlets, pastrami, green beans stay as-is) or sized weight/volume packs (`"16 oz"`, `"6 oz"` stay atomic). `lookupUnitConversion` strips trailing receipt descriptors (`loose`, `sleeve`, `unsalted`, etc.) with `PRICE_PROCESSED_QUALIFIERS` guards so real receipt keys (`garlic loose`, `garlic sleeve`, `romaine`, `farms creamery margarine unsalted`) resolve cleanly while `garlic powder` and `peanut butter` never false-match.
  2. **Dual usable + purchase display (`formatPantryUsableQty`, `fmtPantryUsableAndPurchase`, `fmtPantryNum`, `formatPantryLotHistoryEvent`, `pantryViewHTML`, `styles.css`):** Displays usable quantity per Owner Rule C alongside the purchase quantity (`"5 heads · 1 sleeve"`, `"3 heads, 8 cloves · ⅜ lb"`, `"9 cloves · 0.09 lb"`, `"15 eggs · 1.2 × 12 ct"`). Rule C uses whole units only with `eps = 0.01` before ceiling (`totalSub = ceil(raw × subPerPrimary − 0.01)` split into whole primary + remainder sub-unit omitting zeros; `ceil(raw − 0.01)` when no sub-unit). Applied to card totals (computed once on summed stored qty), individual lot rows, expiring-soon chips, per-lot history lines (`baseKey` threaded into `formatPantryLotHistoryEvent`; stored `ev.delta`/`ev.qtyAfter` unchanged), and unconfirmed-lot confirm inputs (`placeholder="Qty (eggs)"`, `step="1"`). `fmtPantryNum` keeps exact eighths/thirds as Unicode fractions (`½`, `⅜`, `1½`) while rendering non-eighth decimals accurately (`0.09`, `1.2`, `0.94`, `0.83`). Garlic sleeve and garlic loose remain separate cards.
  3. **Whole usable-unit editing & mixed-unit `setPantryTotalQty` (`pantryUsableWholeInputValue`, `pantryUsableToStoredQty`, `setPantryTotalQty`, `wirePantryEvents`):** Lot editors, card-total editors, and unconfirmed-lot confirm inputs accept whole usable units (`step="1"`) and convert back to the stored unit on save (editing garlic heads sets the exact whole-head total, dropping leftover cloves). `setPantryTotalQty` converts per-lot via `convertPantryQty` when an entry contains mixed lot units (e.g. `sleeve` + `lb`).
  4. **Precision fix (`1000` → `10000`):** Stored lot quantities and history deltas now round to 4 decimal places (`10000`) across `normalizePantryLot`, `appendPantryLotHistory`, `setPantryLotQty`, `setPantryTotalQty`, `getPantryHaveFor`, `deductIngredientFromPantryMap`, and `restorePantryDeductionsToMap`. Paired with `eps = 0.01`, every `1..12` egg count on `"12 ct"` and `1..10` garlic head count on `sleeve`/`lb` round-trips cleanly.
  5. **Count-pack recognition & smallest-usable-unit deduction ceiling (`convertPantryQty`, `deductIngredientFromPantryMap`):** `convertPantryQty` integrates `parsePantryCountPackUnit` before `normalizePantryUnit` so `"12 ct"` / `"3 ct"` participate in deductions and conversions. `deductIngredientFromPantryMap` ceilings in the smallest usable unit (`egg` or `clove`) and converts back to the stored unit when a lot resolves via `pantryUsableUnit` (deducting 2 eggs from `1 × 12 ct` leaves 10 eggs / `0.8333`; 3 cloves from `1 sleeve` leaves 4 heads, 7 cloves / `0.94`), while sized weight/volume packs (`"16 oz"` fettuccine) keep atomic whole-pack ceiling deductions.
